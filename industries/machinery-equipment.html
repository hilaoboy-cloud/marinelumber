Industrial Machinery Packaging Capabilities

  • ✓Equipment Handled — Machine tools, industrial presses, pumps, automation equipment, compressors, and heavy fabricated assemblies
  • ✓Transit Considerations — Concentrated weights, irregular footprints, off-center weight distributions, and intermodal transport handling
  • ✓What We Manufacture — Heavy timber skids, fully enclosed wood crates, internal blocking saddles, and hold-down assemblies
