ENERGY, POWER & ELECTRICAL EQUIPMENT PACKAGING

Marine Lumber manufactures custom wood crates, heavy-duty skids, and blocking solutions for commercial EV charging systems, electrical distribution equipment, power transformers, and renewable energy components. Built to protect finished exterior surfaces and sensitive electrical internals throughout transit.

Equipment Packaging & Protection Considerations

  • ✓Equipment Packaged — Commercial EV charging stations, power transformers, switchgear, inverters, battery housings, and electrical infrastructure hardware.
  • ✓Handling & Transit Considerations — Concentrated centers of gravity, finished powder-coat surfaces, sensitive electronics, and ocean/intermodal freight conditions.
  • ✓Manufacturing Solutions — Upright crates, reinforced timber skids, custom internal blocking, cushioning interfaces, and knock-down distribution crates.
  • ✓Surface & Cargo Protection — Purpose-built internal blocking and protective barriers designed to limit equipment movement and surface abrasion.
  • ✓Barrier Packaging — Vapor corrosion inhibitors (VCI) and barrier packaging available for long-distance and ocean transit upon request.
  • ✓Export Compliance — Solid-wood components heat treated and stamped in accordance with ISPM 15 standards for export shipments.
