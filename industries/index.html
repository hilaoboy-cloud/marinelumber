Engineering Rigor

Cross-Sector Engineering & Protection Capabilities

Industrial shipments face severe dynamic stresses during rail humping, rough sea states, and crane transfers. We incorporate advanced engineering into every wood packaging program.

Load & Rigging Engineering

Structural timber cross-members and runner thicknesses are calculated based on equipment weight, footprint, and center of gravity. Fork pockets, crane sling notches, and structural lag-bolting patterns are integrated into the base assembly to ensure safe material handling at every transfer point.

Climate, Shock & Corrosion Defense

For sensitive electronics and export machinery, packaging is outfitted with calculated shock-mount bases, Mil-D-3464 desiccants, and heat-sealed vapor barrier foil or VCI film liners. Cargo arrives at international destinations completely free from rust, oxidation, and harmonic transit damage.

Knock-Down (KD) Kits & Staging

Reduce freight costs and warehouse space by ordering precision pre-cut, flat-pack crate kits. All components arrive pre-drilled, labeled by part number, and bundled with assembly hardware, allowing your packaging crew to assemble crates in minutes right on your production floor.

Process & Execution

From Specification to Production Floor Delivery

A reliable, collaborative engineering process that eliminates transit risk and integrates smoothly into your plant scheduling.

01

Spec & Drawing Review

Submit your 3D CAD models, PDF prints, weight distribution, and destination transit lanes. Our engineering team reviews load profiles and handling requirements.

02

Packaging Architecture

We configure skid timber dimensions, fastener schedules, internal blocking, cushioning foam, and moisture barrier layers tailored to your cargo.

03

Prototype & Fit Sign-off

Pre-production sample crates or component fitment tests are completed to verify dimensions, crane rigging, and assembly ergonomics prior to release.

04

Scheduled Delivery

Volume manufacturing staged across our USA, Brazil, or China facilities, with just-in-time truckload or container shipments delivered to your staging dock.
