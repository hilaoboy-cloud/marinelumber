Defense & Government Packaging Programs

Military Wirebound & Munitions Crates

Engineered wirebound wood crates and heavy-duty plywood containers built for defense logistics, field hardware, munitions packaging, and equipment depot storage. Manufactured to exacting mil-spec drawings, fastener patterns, and structural tolerances.

HHG Lift Vans (Military & Civilian Relocation)

Standard and custom Household Goods (HHG) Lift Vans built for military personnel relocation, diplomatic transfers, and commercial moving/storage operations. High-strength plywood and solid lumber construction offering weather resistance, stacking strength, and flat-pack shipping efficiency.

Defense Packaging Specifications & Capabilities

  • ✓Mil-Spec Wirebound Boxes — M2A1 and M19A1 manufactured in strict compliance with federal specification PPP-B-585 (chemical preservative treatments available)
  • ✓In-House Military Stenciling — standardized DOD marking, NSN stenciling, and custom identification printing performed directly in-plant
  • ✓Thousands of KD Boxes Per Truck — knock-down collapsibility enables high-density freight shipping, absorbing intense shock without friction on metal parts
  • ✓What We Manufacture — wirebound wood crates, heavy-duty skids, plywood transport containers, HHG lift vans, and modular knock-down boxes
  • ✓Extreme Environmental Resistance — engineered to withstand temperature swings, rough depot storage, and repeated forklift handling
  • ✓Global Export Ready — ISPM 15 heat treatment and preservative treatment options available for international deployment
