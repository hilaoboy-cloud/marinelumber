Built on Eight Decades of Experience

Since 1946, Marine Lumber has evolved with the industries it serves — from a Portland lumber supplier to a manufacturer and supplier of industrial wood packaging and related products.

Timeline

From Steamships to Three Continents

1946

Edward S. McGrath founds Marine Lumber Company in Portland, Oregon, supplying lining lumber to maritime freight and steamship operators.

1960s

As intermodal containerization transforms maritime logistics, Stan McGrath transitions operations into industrial packaging, crates and cut-to-size lumber.

Expansion

Headquarters established in Tualatin, Oregon, anchoring manufacturing capacity with modern high-speed milling and automated nailing equipment.

International

Manufacturing footprint expands with facilities in Brazil and China, accompanied by commercial offices in Qingdao and London to serve cross-border export programs.

Today

Third-generation family leadership operating an integrated international packaging manufacturing network under unified quality standards.
