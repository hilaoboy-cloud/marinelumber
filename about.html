Built on Eight Decades of Experience

Since 1946, Marine Lumber has evolved with the industries it serves — from a Portland lumber supplier to a manufacturer and supplier of industrial wood packaging and related products.

Company Heritage

Eight Decades of Industrial Experience.

Founded in 1946 in Portland, Oregon, Marine Lumber began by supplying lining lumber to maritime freight operators. Over three generations of family ownership, the business evolved alongside global shipping modernizations into an established manufacturer of custom industrial crates, production pallets, export dunnage and processed lumber.

Today, managed from Tualatin, Oregon, our international operations provide dependable packaging solutions to manufacturers and exporters worldwide across North America, South America, Europe and Asia.

Timeline

From Steamships to Three Continents

1946

Edward S. McGrath, a Boston maritime lawyer who later moved to San Francisco and Portland (starting Stanley Steamship lines to guide barges through the Columbia River), founds Marine Lumber Company at the height of post-WWII Pacific Northwest shipbuilding, supplying lining lumber and plywood to secure steamship cargo for ocean voyages.

1960s

When intermodal shipping containers arrive and render ocean lining lumber obsolete almost overnight, Edward's son Stan McGrath pivots Marine Lumber to industrial packaging, precision crates, skids, and cut-to-size lumber across the booming Pacific Northwest manufacturing sector.

Expansion

Headquarters established in Tualatin, Oregon, anchoring domestic manufacturing capacity with automated panel saws, high-speed computerized crosscut systems, and automated nailing equipment.

International

Manufacturing footprint expands with facilities in Brazil and China, accompanied by overseas commercial offices in London, England and Qingdao, China to support global supply chains and cross-border export programs.

Today

Operating as a proud 3rd-generation, family-run enterprise with over 75 years of dependable service, supplying defense, agriculture, building products, and heavy industrial machinery worldwide.
