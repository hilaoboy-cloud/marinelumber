Built on Eight Decades of Experience

Since 1946, Marine Lumber has evolved with the industries it serves — from a Portland lumber supplier to a manufacturer and supplier of industrial wood packaging and related products.

Company Heritage

Eight Decades of Industrial Experience.

Founded in 1946 in Portland, Oregon, Marine Lumber began by supplying lining lumber to maritime freight operators. Over three generations of family ownership, the business evolved alongside global shipping modernizations into an established manufacturer of custom industrial crates, production pallets, export dunnage and processed lumber.

Today, managed from Tualatin, Oregon, our international operations provide dependable packaging solutions to manufacturers and exporters worldwide across North America, South America, Europe and Asia.

Company Evolution

Our History & Three Generations of Leadership

1946 1st Generation · Edward Stanley McGrath

Origins in Ocean Steamship Lining Lumber: Marine Lumber Company was founded in 1946 by Edward Stanley McGrath, a Boston maritime lawyer with a background in steamship operations who relocated to the Pacific Northwest. The company name originated because it began as a specialized supplier of lining lumber to the maritime cargo industry — blocking, bracing, and securing bulk goods within ship hulls prior to the arrival of containerized freight.

Late 1960s 2nd Generation · Stanley Robert McGrath

The Container Revolution & Industrial Packaging Pivot: In the late 1960s, Stanley Robert ("Stan") McGrath took over leadership for his father. As intermodal shipping containers emerged and rapidly replaced ship-hull lining lumber, Stan pivoted Marine Lumber into a specialized niche supplier of precision-cut lumber, plywood, industrial crating, and engineered packaging components for Pacific Northwest manufacturing companies — which remained the primary core focus through the early 2000s.

Early 1990s 3rd Generation · Edward Stanley McGrath II

Capacity Scaling & Regional Market Expansion: Edward Stanley McGrath II took the helm in the early 1990s as the third-generation owner. During the 1990s and 2000s, Marine Lumber substantially scaled up factory output capacities, investing in high-speed automated saws, expanded yard staging, and extending crating solutions well beyond the local Northwest into national manufacturing supply chains.

2011 – 2012 Global Footprint · Sherwood Plant & Qingdao Facility

Sherwood Plant & Asia-Pacific Manufacturing Hub: To serve global customers and expand production footprint, Marine Lumber opened a second 50,000 sq. ft., 3-acre manufacturing facility in Sherwood, Oregon. In 2011–2012, the company established a 150,000 sq. ft. (5-acre) high-quality crate manufacturing and cut-to-size plywood facility in Qingdao, China, alongside a sourcing inspection office in Linyi and a purchasing office in downtown Qingdao (the world's 7th largest port city), entering the military household goods lift-vans program.

2018 South American Operations · Santa Catarina, Brazil

High-Capacity South American Sourcing & Export: Marine Lumber established its dedicated manufacturing facility in Santa Catarina, Brazil. Operating close to premier timber and plywood mills, the Brazil plant produces military-certified household goods moving crates, high-volume munitions boxes, and imports hundreds of ocean containers annually into the Oregon hub for Pacific Northwest distribution, generating $5M to $6M USD in annual revenue.

Today Multi-Continental Capability & Resilience

Supply Chain Redundancy & ISO Quality: Today, Marine Lumber operates with over 100 dedicated employees across North America, South America, and Asia. Under Edward Stanley McGrath II's leadership, the company offers a unique advantage: as the only U.S.-based industrial crating manufacturer with both domestic and offshore facilities, we provide resilient pricing stability and flexible multi-continental production capable of shifting volume across continents to insulate customers from tariff, lumber, or freight spikes.
