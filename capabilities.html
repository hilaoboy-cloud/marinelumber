Manufacturing Capabilities Built Around Your Requirements

From initial drawing review to multi-facility production, our engineering discipline, automated machinery and certified export compliance provide dependability across your supply chain.

Global Manufacturing

Operating facilities in Tualatin and Sherwood (Oregon, USA), Santa Catarina (Brazil), and Qingdao (China) under aligned manufacturing standards — producing packaging closer to origin points to optimize freight and delivery timelines.

Packaging Development

Packaging engineered around product geometry, concentrated mass, center of gravity, transit dynamics and lifting methods to prevent damage without excess tare weight.

Export Compliance

Certified heat treatment and compliant ISPM 15 stamping for solid-wood packaging items, facilitating hassle-free customs clearance at international ports.

Material Processing & High-Volume Saws

Computerized bandsaws, automatic chop saws, high-speed rip saws, and panel sizing centers holding tight tolerances across high-volume production batches.

ISO 9001 Quality System

Formal quality assurance system patterned after ISO 9001-2015, integrating QA, engineering, R&D and supply chain continuous improvement across all global facilities.

Advanced Production Equipment & Plant Capacity

Automated Saws & Milling Lines

Computerized bandsaws, automatic chop saws, high-speed rip saws, and panel sizing centers holding tight tolerances down to ±0.2 mm.

Custom Crate Assembly Bays

Dedicated assembly lines equipped with high-pressure pneumatic nailing systems, staging bays for oversized equipment crates, and custom dunnage machining.

In-House Cushioning Fabrication

Dedicated foaming and CNC die-cutting workshop producing custom EPE, EPP inserts, air column bags, and anti-static barrier bags for electronics and medical gear.
