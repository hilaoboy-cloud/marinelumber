Our story

Three Generations. One Promise: It Arrives Intact.

In 1946, Edward S. McGrath, a maritime lawyer, founded Marine Lumber Company in Portland, Oregon, supplying lining lumber to steamship lines. When shipping containers changed the industry in the 1960s, his son Stan moved the company into industrial packaging and crating. Today the third generation leads the company from Tualatin, Oregon, with factories in the USA, Brazil and China. The business has changed. The promise hasn’t.
