Company Heritage

Eight Decades of Industrial Packaging Experience.

Founded in 1946 in Portland, Oregon, Marine Lumber began by supplying lining lumber to maritime freight operators. Over three generations of family leadership, our operations evolved into an integrated international manufacturer of custom industrial crates, production pallets, export dunnage, and processed lumber for manufacturers worldwide.
