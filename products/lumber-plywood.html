Cut-to-Size Lumber, Timber & Panels

We supply and process lumber, timber, plywood and other panel products for packaging, manufacturing and industrial applications.

Material & Processing Specifications

Solid Wood Products
Dimensional softwood lumber, structural hardwood & heavy timbers
Engineered Panels
Softwood & hardwood plywood, OSB, MDF, particleboard
Processing Capabilities
Precision cross-cutting, ripping, panel sizing, CNC shaping
Supply Channels
Domestic regional inventory and established imported supply
Phytosanitary Standards
Heat treatment and ISPM 15 compliance for solid lumber items
Order Scale
Project-specific cut lists through continuous truckload deliveries

Product Materials & Processing Scope

  • ✓Lumber and timber processing — cut-to-length and precision-ripped softwood and hardwood lumber for packaging and fabrication
  • ✓Plywood panels — CDX, structural, ACX, sanded and hardwood plywood sized to custom dimensions for crating and manufacturing
  • ✓OSB, MDF & particleboard — industrial-grade engineered panels cut for crate sheathing, furniture components and separation boards
  • ✓Custom cutting — automated computerized saws and panel saws producing consistent parts to specified project drawings
  • ✓Domestic & imported supply — domestic inventory complemented by international mill direct supply across North America, South America and Asia
  • ✓Supply chain integration — recurring delivery schedules supporting manufacturing plant production without stockouts
