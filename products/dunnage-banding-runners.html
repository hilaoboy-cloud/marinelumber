Dunnage, Blocking & Banding Runners

Secure cargo with wood blocking, bracing and runners manufactured to your required dimensions. Where applicable, solid-wood components can be heat treated and ISPM 15 marked for export.

Cargo Securing Specifications

Species & Grades
Industrial softwood, hardwood timbers & structural lumber
Custom Profiling
Precision routed banding grooves, bevels, chamfers & notches
Dimensions
Cut-to-length timbers from 2x4 up to large-dimension timbers
Export Compliance

Intermodal ocean containers, flatbeds, railcars & export crates
Supply Program
Bundled job-lot packages or recurring millwork inventory

Technical Features & Purpose-Built Protection

  • ✓Blocking and bracing function — prevents dynamic shifting, longitudinal surge and cargo chafing inside containers, railcars and truck trailers
  • ✓Banding groove runners — routed grooves recess steel or poly strapping beneath the wood face, preventing strap abrasion and ensuring smooth conveyor transfer
  • ✓Custom cross-sections and lengths — produced to exact equipment clearances, elimination of on-site trimming on customer loading docks
  • ✓ISPM 15 heat treatment & stamping — verified phytosanitary heat treatment and stamping that helps shipments meet import requirements worldwide
  • ✓Heavy timber capability — heavy blocking manufactured from structural timbers for machinery chocking and concentrated weight transfer
  • ✓Pre-cut kit assembly — packaged in grouped bundles for rapid deployment on your production or packing lines
