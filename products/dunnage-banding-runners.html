DUNNAGE, BLOCKING & BRACING

Cut-to-size wood components used to block, brace and secure cargo during handling and transportation. Manufactured to customer cut lists, dimensions, and program requirements.

Cargo Securing Specifications

Materials
Industrial softwood, hardwood timbers, and structural lumber
Custom Profiling
Banding grooves, chamfers, bevels, notches, and pre-drilled holes
Dimensions
Cut-to-length lumber and heavy timbers from 2x4 up to large timbers
Export Treatment
Heat treatment and ISPM 15 marking available for solid wood
Application Use
Ocean containers, flatbeds, railcars, and export packaging
Supply Programs
Bundled job-lot packages or recurring millwork inventory

Key Manufacturing Capabilities

  • ✓Blocking and bracing components — cut-to-size wood blocks, wedges, and struts used to limit cargo movement inside ocean containers, railcars, and truck trailers
  • ✓Banding-groove runners — precision-routed grooves recess steel or poly strapping beneath the wood face to help prevent strap damage and improve conveyor transfer
  • ✓Custom cross-sections and lengths — produced to specified equipment clearances to eliminate on-site trimming on loading docks and packing stations
  • ✓ISPM 15 heat treatment & marking — phytosanitary heat treatment and official stamping available for solid-wood dunnage in international transit
  • ✓Heavy timber capability — heavy blocking and chocks manufactured from structural timbers for machinery securement and concentrated weight distribution
  • ✓Pre-cut kit assembly — packaged in grouped cut-list bundles for fast deployment on customer packing lines

Manufacturing & Profiling Process

01

Requirements Review

We review cargo dimensions, weight, transit mode, and blocking requirements.

02

Material Selection

Lumber species and dimensions are selected based on load and handling conditions.

03

Processing & Profiling

Lumber is crosscut to length, grooved, chamfered, or drilled according to drawings.

04

Marking & Delivery

Heat treatment verification and ISPM 15 stamping applied where required, followed by bundled delivery.
