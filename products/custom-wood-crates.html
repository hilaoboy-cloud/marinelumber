Custom Wood Crates for Industrial Equipment

We manufacture custom wood crates around your product’s dimensions, weight, handling requirements and transportation method. From individual builds to recurring production programs, we work from your specifications to develop practical domestic and export packaging solutions.

Specification & Capabilities

Materials
Solid lumber, plywood, OSB, engineered combinations
Dimensions
Manufactured to specified product dimensions
Load Capacity
Engineered to specified cargo weight
Order Volume
Single unit prototypes to recurring volume programs
Export Treatment
ISPM 15 heat treatment & official stamp on request
Handling Features
Integrated skids, lifting points & forklift access

Key Features & Engineering Disciplines

  • ✓Engineered to your requirements — designed from CAD models, physical dimensions, weight distribution and handling points
  • ✓Solid wood, plywood or combination construction tailored to product fragility and transport mode
  • ✓Integrated skids and handling access — engineered base structures for forklift entry, rigging and strapping
  • ✓Export packaging options — heat-treated and ISPM 15-marked solid wood for compliant customs clearance worldwide
  • ✓Internal blocking and bracing — custom wood saddles, cushioning mounts and securing members to eliminate transit movement
  • ✓Individual projects and recurring programs — flexible manufacturing supporting one-off heavy equipment or continuous production runs

Manufacturing & Engineering Process

01

Technical Review

We analyze equipment dimensions, weight, fragility rating, handling points and transport route.

02

Packaging Design

Our team develops structural specifications including base member sizing, fastener patterns and clearance margins.

03

Precision Manufacturing

Components are processed to specification with automated lumber saws and panel sizing equipment.

04

Quality & Delivery

Heat treatment stamping applied where required, followed by staged delivery or knocked-down dispatch.
