CUSTOM WOOD CRATES

Wood crates manufactured to your product dimensions, weight, handling requirements, and shipping conditions. From one-off machinery crates to recurring production packaging, we manufacture to your specifications for domestic and international transit.

Specification & Capabilities

Materials
Solid wood, plywood, and combination construction
Dimensions

Built around cargo weight and handling requirements

Key Manufacturing Capabilities

  • ✓Built to customer specification — manufactured from customer drawings, physical dimensions, weight distribution, and handling points
  • ✓Solid wood, plywood, or combination construction configured around product protection and shipping requirements
  • ✓Skid bases and handling access — heavy timber runners configured for forklift entry, crane rigging, and banding
  • ✓Export packaging options — heat-treated and ISPM 15-marked solid-wood components for international export compliance
  • ✓Internal blocking and bracing — cut-to-size wood saddles, blocks, and hold-downs designed to limit product movement during handling and transit
  • ✓Flexible production volume — manufacturing support for individual capital equipment projects or scheduled recurring volume

Order & Production Process

01

Requirements Review

We review product dimensions, weight, rigging points, destination, and transit mode.

02

Configuration

03

Customer Approval

Drawings, cut lists, and specifications are confirmed prior to production runs.

04

Manufacture & Delivery
