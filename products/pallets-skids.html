Pallet Engineering & Production Flow

01

Specification Alignment

We review unit dimensions, weight distribution, racking conditions and handling equipment.

02

Component Sizing

Deckboard thickness, stringer notches and fastener schedules are selected for structural integrity.

03

Automated Fabrication

Precision cross-cutting, ripping and high-speed nailing machines assemble uniform pallets and skids.

04

Treatment & Dispatch

Heat treatment cycle verification and export stamping applied prior to customer loading dock delivery.
